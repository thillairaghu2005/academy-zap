3. DARKEN THE INPUT BORDER TOKEN (#D1D5DB -> #9CA3AF)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
What:      Change `--color-input` in app/globals.css from #D1D5DB to a
           darker gray (#9CA3AF) so interactive field boundaries pass
           WCAG 2.2 non-text contrast (3:1 against the #FFFFFF card).
Why it matters:
  - The B8 accessibility review found that the light #E5E7EB/#D1D5DB
    borders are a deliberate Coursera-style look, but input boundaries
    at <3:1 fail the non-text contrast requirement for people with low
    vision.
  - Focus is already compensated with a strong #0056D2 ring, but the
    resting border is the gap.
What it involves:
  - One token change in globals.css (`--color-input`), which cascades
    to every Input/Select/Textarea since they all read the token.
  - Quick verify: rerun typecheck/lint/build; visually check a form.
Note: pure styling, no logic impact. ~5 minutes of work.
